Stacks, Queues & Deques

Three restricted lists. The restriction is the point: by only allowing access at the ends, every operation becomes O(1) with no shifting and no searching.

Why circular?

If a queue kept front pinned at index 0, every dequeue would have to shift the remaining n−1 elements left — O(n) per removal. A circular array instead moves the index and lets it wrap:

Tracking size (rather than a rear index alone) is what lets you tell a full queue from an empty one — both have front == (front + size) % cap.

Where each one shows up

Stack
call frames, undo/redo, DFS, bracket & tag matching, postfix evaluation, backtracking
Queue
BFS, print/task scheduling, buffers, producer–consumer, level-order traversal
Deque
sliding-window maximum, work stealing, palindrome checks, "undo both ends" editors
