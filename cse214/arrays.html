Arrays & Lists

The same abstract list, two very different implementations. An array gives you O(1) random access but pays for insertions by shifting; a linked list gives you O(1) splicing but has to walk to reach anything.

Why "amortised O(1)"? Doubling means the copies happen at sizes 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 … so the total copying cost after n appends is 1 + 2 + 4 + … + n < 2n. Spread over n appends that is a constant number of copies each, even though a single unlucky append costs O(n). Hit Grow from empty and compare the element copies counter to the size.
